Data from the New York State Senate Open Legislation API, which covers both houses. Senate and Assembly versions of the same bill (“same as”) are shown together, and the stage is the furthest either version reached.

A bill reintroduced in a later session gets a new number. The API links each bill to its earlier versions, and the history under each bill follows those links. Outcomes are shown for sessions back to 2017–18; older versions are listed without an outcome.

Topics are assigned from the committees a bill was referred to and the laws it amends, so a bill can have more than one. Bills withdrawn by their sponsors (“stricken”) and resolutions are left out. The local-government filter also catches bills amending the Real Property Tax Law or Social Services Law, or mentioning counties or municipalities. The sponsor checkboxes appear only for sessions when Sen. Webb or Asm. Kelles was in office. Bills not passed by both houses by December 31 of the second year of the session die and must be reintroduced.
